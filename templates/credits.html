Three of these are ours

We didn't just import libraries for this. We wrote them, published them to PyPI, then used them here.

  • timefuzz reads "back in a couple hours" into a real deadline
  • patchnotes checks our changelog on every push
  • vitalscore runs the START triage on the report form
  • pygeospy geo maths

Why we built it

During Hurricane Harvey, people took their own fishing boats out to pull neighbours off roofs. Some of them didn't come back.

Every app tells you where the flood is. None of them keep a list of who went into it. That's the whole idea, and everything else here is just how we got there.

If you're a judge: the timer on the board is the bit to watch.
