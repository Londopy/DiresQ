Not sure how bad it is?

Four questions. This is START, the same triage protocol used at real multiple-casualty scenes. No training or equipment needed.

Can they walk?
Are they breathing?
Roughly how many breaths per minute? A normal adult is around 12–20.
Can you feel a pulse at their wrist?
Can they follow a simple instruction? Ask them to squeeze your hand.
